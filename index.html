How we work

A coach, not a course library

You can watch design tutorials for a year and still produce work that looks slightly off, because nobody ever told you that your line height is too tight and your palette has no contrast.

Creativax is built around critique. Weekly work, weekly specific notes, weekly revision. It's slower than binge-watching a course and it's the only thing that reliably changes how someone's work looks.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

I'm self-taught. Is that a problem?

No. Most of our clients are. Employers and clients look at work and reasoning, not at where you studied.

Which software do I need?

Figma's free tier covers interface work. For print and branding, any vector tool works — including free ones. We sell nothing and take no affiliate fees.

Will this get me a design job?

It prepares you: better work, better case studies, better outreach. Nobody can promise a job, and we won't.

Graphic design or UI? I'm not sure.

Start with type and layout — it underpins both. We'll help you choose once you've tried each for a few weeks.

How much time does it take?

Six to eight hours a week, most of it making things. The sessions are short; the work is the point.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Want honest notes on your work?

Twenty-five minutes on your portfolio, free, with the one change that would lift everything.
