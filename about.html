Why critique is the whole thing

How we work

Small briefs, weekly notes

Design improves through iteration under feedback. Watching someone else design is enjoyable and does very little, which is why people finish courses and produce the same work as before.

So every program runs on a weekly loop: a small brief, work submitted, specific written critique, revision. Spacing and typography come first because they account for most of the visible gap between amateur and professional work.

Case studies come at the end. Once the work is good, the job is explaining your reasoning — which is what clients and hiring managers are actually buying.

Our principles

Three beliefs behind the method

Specific over encouraging

“Nice work” teaches nothing. Kerning, contrast and hierarchy do.

Ship weekly

Small finished pieces beat one ambitious project that never ends.

Explain your choices

Work that can't be defended gets changed by whoever speaks loudest.
